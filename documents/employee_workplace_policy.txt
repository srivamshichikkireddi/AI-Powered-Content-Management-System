Corporate Employee Benefits and Workplace Code of Conduct Policy

Author: People Operations & Human Resources Department
Category Expectation: Human Resources

Purpose and Scope:
This workplace guidelines document outlines company policies regarding employee health insurance, parental leave, annual vacation accrual, and workplace conduct standards for all full-time and contract personnel.

Employee Health & Wellness Benefits:
All eligible employees receive comprehensive healthcare insurance covering medical, dental, and vision care starting from their first day of active employment.
The company provides a mental health wellness stipend and subsidizes annual fitness memberships.

Paid Time Off (PTO) and Family Leave:
Full-time staff accrue twenty days of paid vacation annually, alongside twelve paid public holidays.
New parents are entitled to sixteen consecutive weeks of fully paid parental leave following the birth or adoption of a child, supporting family bonding and work-life balance.

Professional Conduct and Compliance:
All team members are expected to maintain an inclusive, respectful, and harassment-free work environment.
Any workplace grievances, discrimination, or compliance violations should be reported immediately to Human Resources or submitted anonymously through our confidential reporting hotline.
